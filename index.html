Minh Triet Pham

Hi! I'm Minh Triet Pham, a passionate Data Science student at the University of Melbourne with a strong foundation in Python, machine learning, and full-stack development. I enjoy solving real-world problems with data and have built impactful projects ranging from accident risk prediction to aircraft boarding optimization. My hands-on experience spans across teaching Python to high school students, analyzing data for career platforms, and developing user-friendly websites. Outside of tech, I’m a huge sports enthusiast — I was an active member of both my high school’s basketball and football teams, and I bring that same team spirit and discipline into my work.

Looking ahead, I’m especially driven to explore the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and education. I’m inspired by how AI is reshaping the world and feel deeply connected to education thanks to the passionate teachers who’ve guided me throughout my life. My goal is to contribute to innovative solutions that make learning more accessible, engaging, and personalized through the power of AI.
