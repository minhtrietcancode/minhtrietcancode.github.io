Minh Triet Pham

Hi! I’m a second-year Data Science student at the University of Melbourne, with a strong math background from Hanoi - Amsterdam High School for the Gifted. I'm passionate about AI-especially Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Computer Vision-and have hands-on experience building classification and detection models using PyTorch, TensorFlow, and OpenCV.

I also care deeply about education. Thanks to the inspiring teachers I’ve had from secondary school to UniMelb, I now run a small class where I teach basic computing and Python to five students. I’ve been fortunate to learn from amazing tutors like Jiwon Bae, Thomas Hammer, and fellow Vietnamese educators Dr. Bach Le and Khang Vo, who continue to shape my learning journey.

Outside of tech and teaching, I’m a big fan of sports. I used to play on both the basketball and football teams in high school, and I still enjoy playing whenever I get the chance, along with a few games of billiards now and then.

20-07-2022: We Made It - Best Performance Ever By A Vietnamese Team !!!...Read More

Together with Bach Tran, Kien Pham, and Minh Dang, we made history by achieving the best performance ever for a Vietnamese team - winning the Meritorious Prize and ranking 8th globally at the International Mathematical Modeling Challenge 2022.

That year's problem was both exciting and challenging: designing a mathematical model to optimize airplane boarding and disembarking. We built models, pipelines, and even coded simulations under intense pressure. I still remember spending 3 days learning Python from scratch just to simulate passenger movements - staying up late, debugging endlessly, and somehow making it work. Looking back, it feels surreal that we actually wrote and ran that code!

This was truly an unforgettable experience - not only for the result but for the teamwork, creativity, and resilience it demanded. It's a milestone that continues to inspire me on my journey. Feel free to see more information here: Vietnamese Best Performance Ever
