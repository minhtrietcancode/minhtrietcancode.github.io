Hi! I’m a second-year Data Science student at the University of Melbourne, with a strong math background from Hanoi - Amsterdam High School for the Gifted. I'm passionate about AI-especially Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Computer Vision-and have hands-on experience building classification and detection models using PyTorch, TensorFlow, and OpenCV.

I’ve been fortunate to learn from amazing tutors like Jiwon Bae, Thomas Hammer, and fellow Vietnamese educators Dr. Bach Le and Khang Vo, who continue to shape my learning journey.

Outside of tech and teaching, I’m a big fan of sports. I used to play on both the basketball and football teams in high school, and I still enjoy playing whenever I get the chance, along with a few games of billiards now and then.
